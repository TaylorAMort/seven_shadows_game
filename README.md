Welcome to the land of the Shattered Blade. Your job as one of the shadows is simple: Retrieve the hilt to the Blade and unite the pieces. 

Nothing is so simple though. 

Journey through the ancient castle where the hilt rests, learn the story of the Blade, it's Shattering, and what will happen when you succeed, or fail. The fate of the realm is in your hands.
